The partial preserved authority but produced an overlong recovery handoff; a recovery-only clarification was followed by a fresh passing recovery run. The seven unaffected observations retain their original instruction snapshot. Tests covered prose-only evidence reuse, a required project check, a calculation/consumer repair, a brief incidental answer, two scope/authority-boundary runs, original-human approval recovery and distinct Presidential advice. Actual tool/command counts, elapsed spans and available token counters are recorded in the efficiency summary. There is no A/B control or production cost-reduction claim. These focused observations do not complete the formal full V1 journey baseline.

For future compatibility claims record environment/version, exact snapshot, model/tools, capability, evidence type, actual results and limits. Repeat critical cases at the claimed baseline. Keep partial/failed/aborted observations. A real host running a mock tracker does not prove live tracker integration; a shaped record does not authenticate human approval.

Full V1 still requires formal observed original-human approval/recovery and journey acceptance under the [rubric](../tests/behavioral/rubric.md), with no unresolved critical failure. Optional integrations may remain unverified when excluded from claims. Packaging or local passing tests never establish universal compatibility, publication authority or production readiness.
